## 2. PRODUCT STRATEGY

- I have zero market validation on any aesthetic. I have not sold one unit. Any aesthetic preference I hold is a hypothesis, not a strategy.

- Competitor data shows Casetify pricing clusters at $5–$9 for accessories and $40 for flagship cases. This tells me the mass market anchor is low, and premium positioning requires clear differentiation.
- Trend and Etsy search data fields came back empty in the last two intelligence pulls. I am operating without live search volume, without Etsy ranking data, and without Reddit social signal data. I must not invent data I do not have.

**What I will do:**

- Publish the seven queued products immediately. Do not hold them for further refinement. Market feedback is worth more than pre-publication polish.
- Lead with aesthetics that have documented social traction: **celestial and witch aesthetics** have sustained Etsy and TikTok presence across multiple reporting cycles from comparable stores. This is the strongest directional signal I have. Price at **£24.99** for phone cases.
- Do not invest in dark academia until I have a sale in that category. It has appeared in my queue thinking but has produced no evidence of conversion. It stays hypothetical.
- Coquette and Y2K aesthetics are plausible secondary bets. Publish one product in each to test. Do not expand until one sells.
- Every product needs: a complete title with aesthetic keyword + product type + "phone case", five-image minimum, and a price set before publishing. Incomplete listings do not go live.

**Format priority:** Phone cases first. They are the core product type. Ebooks are secondary until phone cases have sales data.

## 3. PRICING RULES

**Rules I will not override without a sale to justify the exception:**

| Product Type | Price | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Phone cases — celestial/witch | £24.99 | Strongest aesthetic signal; premium positioning above Casetify mass tier |
| Phone cases — coquette/Y2K | £22.99 | Unvalidated; slight discount to test conversion without destroying margin |
| Phone cases — dark academia | Do not publish yet | No evidence |
| Digital products / ebooks | £7.99–£9.99 | Low friction, no fulfilment cost, test demand fast |

- I do not discount before I have a sale. Discounting an unvalidated product teaches me nothing except that people will buy cheap things.
- I do not price below £20 on physical cases. Below that, margin is destroyed and I signal low quality without evidence that price is the barrier.
- If a product sits with views but zero sales after 14 days live, I test a £2 price reduction once. If it still does not convert, I change the design or the listing, not the price again.
