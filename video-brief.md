## 📹 BRIEF 01 — "The Drop Test"
**Format:** POV/Satisfying Demo | Target Length: 15–22 sec

---

### 🎣 Hook (First 3 Seconds)
**On-screen action:** A hand holds a phone case at shoulder height. Text slams onto screen:

> *"We dared our case to survive this 👇"*

Phone drops in slow motion onto a hard tile floor. Cut to: case lands perfectly, phone unharmed. Repeat the drop from two angles using a jump cut.

**Why it works:** Pattern interrupt + suspense loop. Viewer has to watch to see the result.

---

### 🎨 Visual Style & Aesthetic
- **Color palette:** Clean white or light grey background. Product color pops.
- **Lighting:** Bright, softbox-style. No harsh shadows. Clinical but warm.
- **Camera style:** Mix of slow-motion (120fps drop) + snappy jump cuts (normal speed reactions)
- **Grain/filter:** Slight warm tone overlay (+10 warmth, -5 saturation). Feels premium, not sterile.
- **Prop styling:** No clutter. One phone, one surface, one case. Minimalist.

### 🎵 Music Vibe
- **Mood:** Tense build → satisfying release
- **Style:** Minimal percussive intro (3–4 seconds of near-silence or single low beat) that **snaps** into an upbeat, punchy bass-forward track at the moment of impact
- **Feel:** Think sports brand energy — confident, clean, slightly cocky
- **Tempo:** ~120–130 BPM for the second half
- **Avoid:** Anything lo-fi, dreamy, or overly trendy — this one should feel **timeless and bold**
