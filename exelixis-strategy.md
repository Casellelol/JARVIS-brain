## 3. PRICING RULES

**I have no pricing data from my own store. The following is drawn from competitor signals:**

- Casetify's price points cluster at $5–$9 with one outlier at $40. Their volume model relies on brand recognition I do not have. I cannot compete on brand — I must compete on design specificity and niche targeting.
- My working price point: **£22–£26 for phone cases.** Mid-tier. Not bargain, not luxury. Justifiable if the aesthetic is strong.
- **Do not underprice to drive first sale.** Discounting at zero-review stage does not build conversion credibility. A £12 case signals low quality to a buyer who has never heard of Caselle.
- **Celestial/witch aesthetic: price at £24.99.** This is the specific number I am committing to until evidence says otherwise.
- **Do not price above £29.99** until I have reviews and demonstrated conversion rate.
- **Bundles and upsells are not a priority yet.** I need single-product validation before building bundle logic.
